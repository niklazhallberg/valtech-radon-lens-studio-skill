# Quick Start

You can get started making your own SnapML powered Lenses quickly, regardless of whether you are an ML developer, a Lens creator, or a new user!

## For ML Developers[​**](#for-ml-developers "Copy to clipboard")

You can quickly bring in your existing neural networks into Lens Studio by dragging and dropping a compatible .ONNX or .TFLite file into the `Asset Browser` panel of Lens Studio.

* [Checking your model compatibility](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/compatibility.md)
* Exporting your model for [PyTorch](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-frameworks/export-from-pytorch.md), [Tensorflow](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-frameworks/export-from-tensorflow.md) or [Roboflow](https://universe.roboflow.com/snap-xtu13)
* [Importing and using your model](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-component/ml-component-overview.md)
* [Repository with Training Notebook Examples](https://github.com/Snapchat/snapml-templates)
* [Passing in a cropped texture to your model](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-component/crop-textures.md)
* [Scripting how your ML is run and processed](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-component/scripting-ml-component.md)

If you've built an ML-powered app and would like to explore leveraging Snapchat's global, highly engaged audience to drive discovery and user acquisition, [please get in touch](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfhOKRmKFXrZyH5MlTWyrOnXc5VPhEr8b7NN-rMEEhe8-Tj-g/viewform). For qualified partners, there are both no-cost organic and premium opportunities with SnapML. We look forward to hearing from you!
