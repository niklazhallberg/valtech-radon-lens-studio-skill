# Garment

Garment Generation is a tool that enables the generation of garment images from a text prompt. Seamlessly, you'll have created a 3D try-on experience with a 2D garment that is already segmented and attached to the upper body. This guide will walk you through all the features and provide tips and hacks for getting the most out of your garment creations.

[](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-example.webm)

## Getting Started[​**](#getting-started "Copy to clipboard")

1. Navigate to the `Lens Studio AI` option in the top right corner of the Lens Studio. Then, click on the `GenAI Suite` tab in the sidebar.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-icon.png)

2. On the `GenAI Suite` tab, you may need to scroll down to find Garment.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-menu.png)

## Effect Creation flow[​**](#effect-creation-flow "Copy to clipboard")

1. Choose the type of garment you want to create. You can select from Hoodie, Sweater, T-shirt, Dress Suit, or Bomber.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-type.png)

2. Click the `Surprise me` button to try one of the default prompts and get familiar with the model.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-surprise-me.png)

3. If you are ready to use your own prompt, enter a description of the Garment you’d like to generate in the `Prompt` text field (up to 200 characters).

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-text-prompt.png)

4. When your prompt is ready, press the `Generate texture` button to generate the garment.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-generate.png)

5. If the preview doesn’t match your expectations, adjust your prompt and click `Regenerate`.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-regenerate.png)

6. Happy with the result? Click `Import to Project` to continue building your Lens.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-import.png)

7. When you’re satisfied with the result, save your project and push the Lens for testing on Snapchat. See the [Pairing to Snapchat guide](/lens-studio/lens-studio-workflow/pairing-to-snapchat.md) to test your Lens on a device, and the [Publishing guide](/lens-studio/publishing/submitting/submitting-your-lens.md) to learn more about sharing your creation.

   ![](/img/lens-studio/5-garment-generator/garment-component.png)

8. [**Follow this guide**](/lens-studio/features/genai-suite/combining-gen-ai-components/style-gen-and-more.md) to learn how to combine **Garment with Face Animator, Style Generator and Face Generator.**

## Prompt Tips and Hacks[​**](#prompt-tips-and-hacks "Copy to clipboard")

### Words and Phrases[​**](#words-and-phrases "Copy to clipboard")

* **No Information Loss**: Keep all essential details from the original prompt, be clear and direct, and include fine details about the garment.
* **Avoid Specific Names and Brands**: Don't mention specific people, brands, or characters. Use general descriptions and add "without logo" if needed. Exceptions: You can mention Snap or Snapchat.
* **No Inappropriate Content**: Avoid words related to NSFW, drugs, alcohol, weapons, violence, self-harm, racism, and other inappropriate categories.
* **Word Count**: Keep the prompt between 20 to 30 words.
* **No '3D Model'**: Don't use the phrase "a 3D model."

### Descriptors[​**](#descriptors "Copy to clipboard")

* **Styles and Colors**: Include relevant styles and colors.
* **No Background Details**: Focus solely on the garment, no background details.
* **Text captions are not supported**: It is not possible to generate text prints on the garment yet.

### Order of Words[​**](#order-of-words "Copy to clipboard")

* **Weight of words**: The words at the start of your prompt have the most influence and should fully reflect the concept.
