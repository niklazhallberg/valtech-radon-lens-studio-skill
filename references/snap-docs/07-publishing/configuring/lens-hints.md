# Lens Hints

In Lens Studio you can define a hint to be shown to the user at the startup of your Lens. You can also show a hint when an event occurs or any time depending on your design. This guide will show you how to set up a startup hint.

These predefined hints are localized to various languages, which makes them more user friendly than a [text](/lens-studio/features/text/2d-text.md) component. However, you can also localize your own hint. Take a look at the [Text Localization](/lens-studio/features/text/localization.md) guide to learn more!

## Setup[​**](#setup "Copy to clipboard")

To set a hint for your Lens, go to the `Project Settings` button in the top left of Lens Studio and click it.

In the `Project Settings` window, select the `Mobile` tab on the left side bar, and select the `Camera Hint` drop-down to the desired Lens hint.

For more information on the `Project Settings` window, please refer to the [Configuring Project Settings](/lens-studio/publishing/configuring/configuring-project-info.md) guide.

![](/assets/images/lens-hint-527d042333bde9e2d000f7eb5822d763.png)

Hints will be shown to a user each time your Lens is activated by the user. They fade away after a moment. Hints are suggested when your Lens has some level of interactivity
