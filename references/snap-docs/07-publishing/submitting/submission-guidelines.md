# Submission Guidelines

## Lens Studio Submission Guidelines[​**](#lens-studio-submission-guidelines "Copy to clipboard")

Lens Studio empowers our community to create expressive, immersive AR experiences, and then distribute their creations to Snapchatters all over the world. Lenses empower people to express themselves, live in the moment, learn about the world, and have fun together.

All Lenses submitted are subject to Snap’s [Terms of Service](https://www.snap.com/terms/), [Community Guidelines](https://support.snapchat.com/a/guidelines) and the [Lens Studio Terms](https://snap.com/en-US/terms/lens-studio-license-agreement/), and must follow all Snap policies governing the use of our products or services. Please also review our [Privacy Policy](https://www.snap.com/privacy/privacy-policy/) to learn how we handle information when you use the Services. You agree that we can collect, use, and share your information as described in that policy and any updates to that policy. We may update our terms, policies, and guidelines from time to time, so please review them regularly.

If you would like to run AR ad campaigns on Snapchat, please visit our [Business Center](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/) and this page for [Submitting your Lens](/lens-studio/publishing/submitting/submitting-your-lens.md).

## Tips for Lens Creators[​**](#tips-for-lens-creators "Copy to clipboard")

Snapchatters can discover your Lenses through the Lens Carousel, Lens Explorer, other areas of the Snapchat app, and [Camera Kit](https://kit.snapchat.com/camera-kit).

### Kind[​**](#kind "Copy to clipboard")

* Be inclusive! Your Lens should be functional for all Snapchatters, regardless of skin tone, face or body shape or size, language, ability, race, color, caste, ethnicity, national origin, political affiliation, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, veteran status, immigration status, socioeconomic status, age, weight or pregnancy status. In short: be respectful and appropriate for our global, 13+ community.

### Smart[​**](#smart "Copy to clipboard")

* Be particularly thoughtful about privacy and safety; avoid creating Lenses that have the potential to cause oversharing of your own or someone else’s sensitive information, such as a home address or personal phone number.

### Creative[​**](#creative "Copy to clipboard")

* Be visually compelling in a way that makes use of the camera. Make something that Snapchatters will want to experience and share with their friends.
* Experiment! We are constantly adding new capabilities to Lens Studio to expand creative possibilities.

## Intellectual Property Rights[​**](#intellectual-property-rights "Copy to clipboard")

* You must obtain all necessary rights and permissions for the content you submit as part of a Lens. This includes obtaining all necessary performance rights for music included in Lenses, and paying all applicable royalties for the music.
* Rightsholders and their agents can report alleged intellectual property infringement found in Lenses to Snap. Snap takes all complaints regarding intellectual property infringement seriously and will investigate them. Takedowns are processed at Snap’s discretion. We make no guarantees as to the outcome of our investigations or the legitimacy of the complaints we receive. To report copyright infringement on Snapchat, follow the instructions at this [link](https://support.snapchat.com/co/report-copyright).
* Please keep in mind that in making your Lens available to Snapchatters, Snap may display your name, business name (if applicable), Lens name, Snapchat account username or display name, city, state, and country, as submitted by you or tied to your Snapchat account.
* Do not include content that suggests any partnership with or endorsement by Snap, or imitates Snap’s trademarks or products. Prohibited references include (but are not limited to) “Snap,” “Snapchat,” “Spectacles,” “Bitmoji,” the Ghost Logo, the Spectacles Logo, and the Snapchat user interface.

## Technical Requirements[​**](#technical-requirements "Copy to clipboard")

* **Frame Rate:** For iPhone 6 and higher, the Lens should run at 27 FPS or higher. For Android Galaxy S6 and higher, the Lens should run at 15 FPS or higher.
* **Memory:** In Snapchat, the Lens should consume a maximum of 150 MB RAM memory.
* **Exported Lens Size:** Once exported, the zipped Lens should be no more than 8 MB. [ML models](/lens-studio/features/snap-ml/ml-overview.md) are counted separately, with a limit of 10mb.
* **Texture Size:** No texture should exceed 2048 x 2048 resolution. No mesh should exceed 100,000 triangles, or 60,000 triangles for meshes with joints/skinning for best performance.
* **Lens icon and preview media:** We encourage original imagery.

For additional resources please visit the [Lens Studio Site](https://ar.snap.com/lens-studio).
