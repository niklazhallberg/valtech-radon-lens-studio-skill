# Overview

You can track bodies and their movement in Lenses, whether it be in 2D screen space, 3D, and more.

## Tracking the Body[​**](#tracking-the-body "Copy to clipboard")

There are several ways you can detect the body and its various parts. You can track the body in space, in the camera texture, and more.

[![](/img/lens-studio/overview-icons/3D_Full_Body_Tracking.svg)](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/world/object-tracking.md)

[2D Body Tracking](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/world/object-tracking.md)

[Using the Object Tracking 2D component, you can track the first body and various joints, in 2D space for more efficient tracking. You can even be more efficient by only tracking the upper body.](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/world/object-tracking.md)

[![](/img/lens-studio/overview-icons/3D_Body_Tracking_Asset.svg)](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/object-tracking-3d.md)

[3D Body Tracking](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/object-tracking-3d.md)

[Using Object Tracking 3D component, you can track one or more bodies and various joints in 3D space.](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/object-tracking-3d.md)

[![](/img/lens-studio/overview-icons/Segmentation.svg)](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/fullscreen-segmentation.md)

[Body Segmentation](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/fullscreen-segmentation.md)

[You can segment, i.e cut out, or green screen, the body or its parts to make various visual effects.](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/fullscreen-segmentation.md)

[![](/img/lens-studio/overview-icons/BodyDepthTexture.svg)](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/person-normals-and-depth.md)

[Body Normals and Depth Texture](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/person-normals-and-depth.md)

[You can get a texture of the body which provides information on how far it is from the camera, as well as the surface direction of each pixel of the body.](/lens-studio/features/ar-tracking/body/segmentation/person-normals-and-depth.md)
