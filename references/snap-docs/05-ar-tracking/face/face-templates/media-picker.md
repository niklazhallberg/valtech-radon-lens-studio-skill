# Media Picker

The [Media Picker Texture](/lens-studio/assets-pipeline/2d/media-picker-texture.md) enables a Lens to display an image or video from the user’s device. The `Image Picker Collage` asset shows how to place the media in a designated area.

![](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-preview.png)

## Image Picker Collage Asset[​**](#image-picker-collage-asset "Copy to clipboard")

Find the `Image Picker Collage` asset in the Asset Library and import it into your project. Click [here](/lens-studio/assets-pipeline/asset-library/asset-library-overview.md#prefab) to learn more about how to use assets in the Asset Library. Once you import the asset from Asset Library, you can find the package in the Asset Browser. Follow the instructions and drag the prefab `Image Picker Collage` into Scene Hierarchy undert [`Orthgraphic Camera`](/lens-studio/lens-studio-workflow/scene-set-up/camera.md#orthographic-camera) to create a new Scene Object. You can right-click and select `Unpack for Editing` from the drop-down menu to unpack all assets stored in the bundle.

![](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-import-asset.png)

In the `Preview` panel, click on the `Screen Image` to open the media picker. Then choose the media to show the media in the `Screen Image`.

[](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-choose-media.webm)

Click the `Screen Image` again to reselect the media.

[](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-click-to-choose.webm)

Click on the `Image Picker Collage__PUT_IN_ORTHO_CAM` to adjust settings in the `Inspector` panel.

![](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-collage-controller.png)

Click on each child of the `Image Picker Collage__PUT_IN_ORTHO_CAM` and follow the instructions to apply changes. Double click on the child to open `2D Scene` panel and move the `Screen Image` around.

![](/img/lens-studio/5-features/media-picker-2d-editor.png)

## Previewing Your Lens[​**](#previewing-your-lens "Copy to clipboard")

You’re now ready to preview your Lens experience. To preview your Lens in Snapchat, follow the [Pairing to Snapchat](/lens-studio/lens-studio-workflow/previewing-your-lens.md) guide.
