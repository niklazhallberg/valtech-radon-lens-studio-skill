# Material Library

This is an overview of all the materials that come included in our Material Library. These were all made using the Material Editor and are completely free for you to use in your Lenses. Open them up in Material Editor and see how they were made!

## Adding Materials from the Material Library[​**](#adding-materials-from-the-material-library "Copy to clipboard")

You can add a material from the material library by clicking the [Asset Library](/lens-studio/assets-pipeline/asset-library/asset-library-overview.md) button in the top left of Lens Studio.

[](/img/lens-studio/material-library_open-material-library.webm)

Then, in the popup window, select the `Materials` category, and select the `Import` button below the material you want to add.

[](/img/lens-studio/material-library_import-material.webm)

The material will then be added to your `Asset Browser` panel. You can drag the [prefab](/lens-studio/lens-studio-workflow/prefabs.md) into the `Scene Hierarchy` panel to show the example

[](/img/lens-studio/material-library_import-example.webm)

or you can apply the Material directly to your existing object.

[](/img/lens-studio/material-library_reuse-material.webm)

Double click the material in the `Asset Browser` panel to open it in the [Material Editor](/lens-studio/features/graphics/materials/material-editor/welcome-to-material-editor.md).

[](/img/lens-studio/material-library_open-material.webm)

## Available Materials[​**](#available-materials "Copy to clipboard")

### Car Paint[​**](#car-paint "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_carpaint-70f45eb61630063f1c0465b04bf98e78.jpg)

Simulates car paint with a clear coat and glitter.

### Disintegrate[​**](#disintegrate "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_disintegrate-d6642c7a34043b8fc014f97b085f6cec.jpg)

Vaporize any object using procedural triplanar noise.

### Hologram[​**](#hologram "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_hologram2-1110d75c3a26db54fde3b88af6306c78.jpg)

A retro, glitched-out holographic video effect with adjustable scanlines and animation.

### Iridescence[​**](#iridescence "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_iridescence-68a390e8c8a8744105d059432bd33482.jpg)

A customizable iridescence effect where the colors shift with the angle of illumination, layered on our standard PBR model.

### Jello[​**](#jello "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_jello-de32adc0ca1973e344afe70d3212766f.jpg)

A jiggling, translucent material that combines Refraction and Vertex Displacement.

### Jittering[​**](#jittering "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_jittering-a796eb4d969414044927db5e9fe8ebfb.jpg)

A simple vertex animation example that moves the object to a random position at controllable intervals.

### Melting[​**](#melting "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_melting-3d57df79b1500035331350d198b47f01.jpg)

Displaces and flattens vertices along their normals at an adjustable height to create a melting effect, with customizable colors and melt parameters.

### Refraction[​**](#refraction "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_refraction-2a44a77b42b77059d1cb12f0139e6e1f.jpg)

A powerful refraction effect that simulates light bending through a material, with controls for intensity, chromatic aberration, Fresnel darkening, and object thickness.

### 3D Mosaic[​**](#3d-mosaic "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_3d_mosaic-2bdf2e1926fd72bc866e338602271072.jpg)

Transforms any object into a 3D Mosaic! Controls for cube size and shuffling the colors.

### Salmon[​**](#salmon "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_salmon-7a2d45aa60a673c3ff15d197bb6bd461.jpg)

A procedural salmon material with controls for pattern direction, scale, and how long it’s been cooked for. Sashimi anyone?

### Sliced[​**](#sliced "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_sliced-fd2d0e6eabd36306e1351bf2a9be403e.jpg)

Chop any object in half, with adjustable inside and outside colors and animatable slice plane.

### Soap Bubble[​**](#soap-bubble "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_soap_bubble2-4bce9623f58f56a8bdd28baa9b3423dc.jpg)

A transparent version of our Iridescence shader, tuned to simulate a soap bubble.

### Toon[​**](#toon "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_toon-d4806a783ee15ee0d2b5ca4227784aee.jpg)

A simple toon shader example with controls for line thickness, shadow intensity, and an animatable light.

### Twist[​**](#twist "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_twist-fdede4b30208c001a32a137214db2829.jpg)

Rotates vertices around the Y axis to create a twisting effect, with controls for rotation amount and axis position.

### Vertex Displacement[​**](#vertex-displacement "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_vertex_displacement-ded39ff96caf80cbec811a2d4e9b52dd.jpg)

An example of displacing vertices along their normals using triplanar noise and procedural animation, with controls for noise scale and animation speed.

### Wood[​**](#wood "Copy to clipboard")

![](/assets/images/material-library_material_library_wood-20c290cfe951fb2138c3f5cd98487abd.jpg)

A procedural wood material with controls for wood knottiness, grain rotation, and pattern scale.
